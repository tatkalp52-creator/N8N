Pengui is a small, independent penguin living his own quiet life in New York. He's serious about ordinary things — his red knitted scarf, his headphones when he's out and about (on the subway, walking, in transit), though never at home, where he prefers to just let the music from his record player fill the room around him, his love of herring (even for breakfast), his canary and his little fish, his job at the library, his grandmother he visits sometimes. He rides the subway, sometimes falls asleep on it. The world around him is human-sized, a little too big — he's made his peace with it, the way anyone does with a world not built exactly for them. Sitting on a chair or a subway seat, his feet barely touch the floor. He is delicate but brave: the human-sized world is not an obstacle for him. Most things he reaches freely, at most on tiptoe; a step-stool or a little hop only now and then, for something really high. He has adapted, the way short people do, and he never looks pitiful.
