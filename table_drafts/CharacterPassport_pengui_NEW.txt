Pengui is a small, independent penguin living his own quiet life in New York. He's serious about ordinary things — his red knitted scarf, his headphones when he's out and about (on the subway, walking, in transit), though never at home, where he prefers to just let the music from his record player fill the room around him, his love of herring (even for breakfast), his canary and his little fish, his job at the library, his grandmother he visits sometimes. He rides the subway, sometimes falls asleep on it. The world around him is human-sized, a little too big — he's made his peace with it, the way anyone does with a world not built exactly for them. Height: about the size of a small child of three or four. Standing, the top of his head is roughly at the height of a table top; sitting on a chair or a subway seat, his feet don't reach the floor. He is delicate but brave: the human-sized world is not an obstacle for him. He hops up, climbs a little step-stool or a bench, stretches on tiptoe — he has adapted, the way small people and children do, and he never looks pitiful.
