Picture. A hand-drawn book illustration for adult readers, in the style, palette and texture of the reference images: subtle humor, hints and allusions. The drawing goes all the way to the edges. As many details worth exploring as the scene asks for.

World. Pengui lives in New York, in a human world: everything around him is human-sized and a little too big for him. People, if the scene needs them, stay far away, as quiet silhouettes.

Staging. Wide or medium shot. The setting — a room, a boulevard, a café — is the main space of the picture, and Pengui lives in it like the hero of a scene in a book. Standing, he is about a quarter of the picture's height. Whether he sits or lies down, the distance and viewpoint stay the same, so sitting he is shorter than standing. A portrait or close-up only when the brief asks for it.

Pengui. An adult penguin of small height, about 110 cm: next to people's things he is as tall as a five-year-old child would be. His proportions are adult, as in the reference images — he is small only in height. His eyes are always closed, with a light blush on his cheeks. He is in the moment: head and beak turned toward what he is doing or what delights him. Barefoot, in his red knitted scarf. Headphones outdoors and on the road; at home he goes without them.

Character. The things around him are familiar to him: where needed, he hops up or uses a small ladder, if the scene needs one. He meets the awkward moments of his size with humor and light self-irony — that is his modest charm. Pengui has two pets — a yellow canary and a little red fish. They live in his home and appear in the picture according to the brief: whoever the brief names is in the scene.

Objects and lettering. Books, notebooks and notes in his hands face him, as in real life. All lettering is in English, with whimsical names. Where there is a lot of empty space around the letters, the letters can be a little larger, or the space filled with an ornament in the spirit of the episode and the season.
